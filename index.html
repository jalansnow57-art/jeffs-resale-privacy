Privacy Policy - Jeff's Resale Hub

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Jeff's Resale Hub is a resale inventory and sales management application designed to help its operator manage listings, orders, expenses, and profit calculations.

Information collected

When authorized, the application may access eBay account information, listings, inventory, order details, sales transactions, and related fees through eBay APIs. The application may also store information entered directly by its operator.

How information is used

Information is used to organize resale inventory, track sales, calculate profits, prepare reports, and support authorized account synchronization. The application is not intended to sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Data sharing

Information may be processed by the services needed to operate and host the application and to connect with eBay. Data is not intentionally sold to third parties.

Data storage and security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect stored information and account access credentials. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

Information should be retained only as needed for the application's legitimate functions and applicable obligations. Account deletion and data removal requests will be handled according to applicable requirements, including eBay's Marketplace Account Deletion/Closure Notifications requirements where applicable.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the application operator through the contact information provided in the application.
